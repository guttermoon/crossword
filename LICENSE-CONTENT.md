# What covers what

This repository holds four different kinds of thing, and one licence cannot
sensibly cover all of them.

## The code — MIT

`index.html`, everything under `css/`, `js/` and `scripts/`, and the build
configuration. See [LICENSE](LICENSE). Take it, change it, ship it.

The article, the 39 clues, their footnotes and the source list: everything in
`data/gazette.js`, `data/puzzles.js` and `copy/`, and the booklet built from
them in `print/`.

Shrikhand, Courier Prime, Inter and Libre Franklin, in `fonts/`. The licence
travels with them: see [fonts/OFL.txt](fonts/OFL.txt).
